# Brand

- **Channel name:** Spears & Tears
- **Handle:** @SpearsAndTears (to be checked on YouTube)
- **Tagline:** Ancient wars. Real history. Way too much crying.
- **Mascot / narrator:** Sobius — a small, perpetually crying Roman-style soldier with a bronze helmet, red crest and a spear.
  He appears in every episode to react to the chaos (the "tears" in Spears & Tears).
- **Font:** Luckiest Guy (SIL Open Font License, `assets/fonts/`).
- **Palette:** sand `#f6dc95` → `#d39a43`, ground `#a67a3d`, outline `#1a1a1a`, skin `#f2ece0`, Roman red `#c0392b`, bronze `#c4913a`, tears `#5db7ee`.
- Regenerate images: `node assets/brand/export.js`
