# TikTok plan — Spears & Tears

Files: `animation/previews/tiktok/` (1080x1920, 30 fps, burned-in English subtitles).
Rebuild: `python3 animation/tools/tiktok.py animation/episodes/ep01/tiktok.json` (and `ep02/tiktok.json`).

Layout is TikTok-safe: no text under the right-hand buttons (x > 940) or in the bottom caption area (y > 1540).
Shorts open on their strongest line (a 3–5 s hook) before the story starts; parts are full chapters, each longer
than one minute (only videos over 1 minute count for the Creator Rewards Program, which needs 10k followers).

## Every post
- Turn on **"AI-generated content"** (the narrator voice is AI: ElevenLabs in ep01, Gemini TTS in ep02).
- No YouTube link or "watch on YouTube" in the caption (TikTok limits reach for that); put the YouTube channel in the bio.
- Pin a comment asking a question (below). Reply to the first comments in the first hour.
- Parts: add every part to a TikTok playlist ("Cannae", "Kadesh") so viewers can binge the series.
