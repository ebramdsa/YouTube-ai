So what do we learn from Cannae? That numbers don't always win. That strategy beats brute force. That one general's genius can matter more than eighty-six thousand soldiers.

But also... that even genius has limits. Even Hannibal couldn't break Rome. Because Rome was more than one city or one army. It was an idea. And ideas are harder to kill than men.
