Years later, Hannibal is a general. War breaks out again. Rome expects to fight in Spain, maybe Africa. Normal places. Hannibal has a different idea.

He's going to march overland. Through the Pyrenees. Across southern France. Over the Alps. In autumn. With war elephants.

It was insane.

He lost half his men on the way. Almost all the elephants died. Soldiers froze. Rockslides buried whole units. But in the end...

...a Carthaginian army appeared in northern Italy. Out of nowhere. Rome didn't see it coming.
