So Rome sent an army to stop him. At the River Trebia, December 218 BC.

Hannibal hid his entire army and waited for the Romans to wade through an icy river before breakfast. By the time they realized they were being attacked, they were already surrounded.

Thousands drowned trying to escape.

Rome tried again the next year. Lake Trasimene, June 217 BC.

Hannibal does the same thing. He hides his army in the hills above a foggy lakeshore. The Romans march straight into the trap. Fifteen thousand Romans die in a few hours. Fifteen thousand more are captured. Their general is killed.

And somewhere during all this, Hannibal marched through a swamp for days. He got an infection. Lost the sight in one eye. But did he go home? No.

He just... put on an eyepatch and kept going.
