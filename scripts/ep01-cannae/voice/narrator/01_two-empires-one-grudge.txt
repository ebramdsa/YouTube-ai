To understand why, you have to know about Rome and Carthage. Two superpowers fighting over the western Mediterranean. One is an empire of farmers and soldiers — stubborn, organized, brutal. The other is a trading empire, wealthy, with the best navy in the world and enough money to hire any mercenary they need.

They already fought once. Twenty-three years of war. Hundreds of thousands dead. And Rome won.

But one Carthaginian general didn't handle losing well. His name was Hamilcar Barca.

And he made a promise to his son at an altar — a promise so strong, it would change history.

That boy was named Hannibal. And Hamilcar told him: 'Never be at peace with Rome.'

Hannibal never forgot.
