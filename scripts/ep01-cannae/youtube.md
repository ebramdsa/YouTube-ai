## Description
In 216 BC, Rome sent the biggest army in its history to crush Hannibal. By sunset, it was gone.

This is the story of Cannae — the battle where a one-eyed Carthaginian general, outnumbered almost two to one, surrounded and destroyed a Roman army of 86,000 men. How did he do it? And why did Rome still win the war?
