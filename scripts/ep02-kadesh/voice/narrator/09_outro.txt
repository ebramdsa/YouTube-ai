So who won the Battle of Kadesh? On the battlefield, probably nobody. Ramesses survived a trap that should have destroyed him. Muwatalli kept Kadesh. And in the end, both of them decided the war wasn't worth it.

But Ramesses won something else. More than three thousand years later, we still know his name, and we still read his version. He didn't just fight the battle. He won the story.
