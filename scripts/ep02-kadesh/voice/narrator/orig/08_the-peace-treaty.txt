But here's the twist. About sixteen years later, Egypt and the Hittites stopped fighting and signed a peace treaty. One of the oldest peace treaties ever found. They promised not to attack each other, to help each other against enemies, and to send back each other's runaways.

The Egyptian copy was carved in hieroglyphs on temple walls. The Hittite copy was written on clay tablets in Hattusa. Archaeologists found both, and they match almost line by line. Except for one detail. Each side says the other side asked for peace first.

Ramesses even married a Hittite princess.

Today, a copy of that treaty is on display at the United Nations in New York. The oldest peace deal, in the home of modern peace deals.
